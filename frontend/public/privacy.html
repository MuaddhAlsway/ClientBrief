Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains how MU.LAB ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when you submit a project brief through our client intake platform.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, company, and location.
  • Project information, including your goals, requirements, references, budget range, and timeline.
  • Reference links you choose to share (for example, inspiration or competitor websites).

How we use your information

  • To review your project brief and respond to your inquiry.
  • To prepare a proposal, scope, and any follow-up communication you request.
  • To maintain records related to our professional relationship.

Legal basis and consent

We process your information based on your request for our services and the consent you provide when submitting the brief. Marketing communication is sent only if you explicitly opt in separately from your project inquiry.

Email delivery

Brief submissions are delivered to the MU.LAB studio by email. We never use your email address as the sender; it is only set as the reply-to address so we can respond to you directly.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may use trusted service providers (for example, email and hosting providers) strictly to operate this platform, and only to the extent necessary.

Retention

We retain your submission for as long as needed to evaluate and act on your inquiry, and for reasonable business record-keeping afterward.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted by contacting us at the email address below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you provide. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email privacy@mulab.example. Replace this address with the studio's real contact email before launch.
